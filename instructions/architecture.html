Parts Intelligence Architecture

A modular monolith in sixteen steps, each a Python module with a typed contract at its boundary. Written against the real files — every row count, date range and column quirk below came from opening them, not from the data dictionary.

Three things the files settled

  • The Payer contradiction was never a contradiction. The column holds both: "26198      Yamaha Sales Center". So does Material. One parser, applied per column per file — orders.xlsx doesn't use this encoding at all.
  • The revenue formula needed no bracket-fixing. Revenue Re and Cost Reduc are zero in all 21,301 rows, and Net Sales = Sales Pric + Discount holds to 0.0. Revenue is Net Sales; margin is Net Sales − Cost.
  • The parc has a four-year hole, and it is real. 2020: 26,701 units. 2021: 85. 2022–24: none. 2025: 22,084. That is the vehicle import ban. The fleet is bimodal — a 2014–20 block now aged 6–12 sitting in the high-consumption band, and a young block that will demand little for years.

Architecture

Rules that hold everywhere

  • One identity per part. active_sku_id resolves the supersession chain. Aggregate on anything else and one physical part's demand splits across its old and new numbers, understating every forecast.
  • Contracts at every boundary. pandera schemas; a violation raises. These are SAP exports whose column meanings are not self-evident — fail closed or propagate garbage.
  • Read each workbook once. 20 MB and 18 MB files converted to parquet up front; every step reads parquet. Re-reading per stage costs minutes per run.
  • Idempotent stages. run(ctx) → StageResult, no module-level mutable state, same input same output. Python modules under src/, never notebooks.
  • The final 12 months are sealed. Split in step 07, opened once in step 13, never re-opened. A holdout you re-optimise against has stopped being a holdout.
  • Every number was computed in a step, tested, and written to a mart. The API computes nothing.

Supersession chain across Material + 10 supersede columns → one active_sku_id. Vector search tolerant of typos; lookup labels every hit QUERIED / CURRENT / OLDER / RELATED. Everything downstream aggregates on this id.

Order Cleaning

46,514 order lines → monthly demand history. Dealer join, C/H split, material category, and the measured Lost Quantity — the most valuable column in the project.

src/demand
Billed sales, revenue and margin. Net Sales = Sales Pric + Discount (verified exact); margin is Net Sales − Cost. Performance by dealer, RM, ASE, province, district.
05

src/demand
Fulfilment classes, supply reliability β̂ with shrinkage for thin parts, and the lead-time distribution — the σ, not just the mean.
06
Classification
src/parts
Behaviour class (service / wear / crash / cosmetic / engine) by trained classifier scored on macro-F1; ADI/CV² quadrants; ABC, XYZ, FSN, criticality. These are routing keys, not reports.
07
Model Selection
src/forecast
Rolling-origin backtest, horizon 4 months. MASE for smooth, pinball for intermittent, never MAPE. Champion per segment. Final 12 months split off and sealed.
08
Material Forecast
src/forecast
Own-history forecast and parc-driven demand, reconciled. λ(p,a) by penalised NNLS pooled on behaviour class, with confidence intervals because cohort collinearity makes single coefficients weak.
09
Unit Sales
src/parc
56,211 VIN-level registrations, 2025–26. Watch the rows where dealer, province and RM all carry the sales-office name.
10
UIO Cohorts
src/parc
Units in operation per model, month and yearly age bucket, rolled forward with age-specific survival. The 2021–24 zero must survive into the age histogram.
11
Targets
src/parc
Unit forecast for active models plus a scenario simulator. A +10% unit target must not produce +10% parts demand — the lag through the age profile is the test that the mechanism is wired up.
12
Stock Position
src/inventory
PDC only (W1B4, 86.6% of units), cleaned against PN_Yamaha. on_hand = Unrestricted — the only bucket the export has. IP = on_hand + on_order − backorders.
13
Policy Engine
src/inventory
Three safety-stock strategies routed by quadrant; fill-rate→z through the unit normal loss, not a CSL table; four monthly-executable policies scored by walk-forward simulation, selected on cost subject to service, proven once on the sealed holdout.
14
Monthly Order
src/inventory
EOQ floor, MOQ, pack rounding, and supply inflation off by default because 1/β̂ is a feedback loop. Every line carries why it was proposed.
15
FastAPI Service
src/api
Parts search, forecasts, parc, policy, order proposal, run triggers. Serves marts, computes nothing, and never writes a PO back to SAP.

Gates

Step 13 is the acceptance gate

Achieved fill rate at or above target per ABC class and average inventory value at or below baseline, on the sealed holdout. A win on one axis only is a frontier to show the owner, not a pass to claim.

Step 01 needs an accuracy report, not a parser

The colour rules are ambiguous by nature. Sample at least 30 parts per PDF layout, verify by eye, and report precision per rule. Anything unresolved goes to exceptions with its raw remark — never guessed into a variant.

Survival may be assumption, not measurement

Without de-registration records, vehicle retirement is not observed. If that is the case, say so and run the policy under at least two survival curves, so the sensitivity is visible rather than buried in a single number.
